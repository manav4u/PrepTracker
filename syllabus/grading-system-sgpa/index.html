SPPU grading system: grades, SGPA and CGPA

How marks become grades, grades become grade points, and grade points become SGPA. All numbers come from the SPPU credit framework handbook for the Faculty of Science and Technology.

The handbook prints the fail row as "Marks of 40 or less", which overlaps the D band (40 to 44). We show it as "below the pass mark" instead of guessing a cutoff. The handbook also labels two bands "B" (B+ for 55 to 59, B for 50 to 54). Check your university exam rules for the exact fail boundary.

SGPA

SGPA is the credit-weighted average of grade points over every graded course in the semester:

SGPA = sum(grade points x credits) / total credits

Each graded row counts with its own credits, theory and term work or practical alike, the way the handbook lists them.

Worked example (made-up numbers)

RowCreditsGradePointsCredits x points
Theory A3A+927
Tutorial A1A88
Theory B2B+714
Practical B1O1010
Total759

SGPA = 59 / 7 = 8.43. This example is ours. The handbook's own sample table shows 98 credit points over 22 credits as an SGPA of 8.9, which does not add up, so we did not copy it.

CGPA and class of degree

CGPA is calculated the same way as SGPA, over all semesters of the programme. If you clear a failed course or improve a grade, the new grade replaces the old one in both SGPA and CGPA. The class awarded depends on CGPA:

CGPAClass
9.50 or moreOutstanding (O)
8.50 to below 9.50Excellent (A+)
7.50 to below 8.50Very Good (A)
6.25 to below 7.50Good (B+)
5.25 to below 6.25Above Average (B)
4.75 to below 5.25Average (C)
4.00 to below 4.75Pass (D)
Below 4.00Fail (F)

We do not give a CGPA to percentage conversion. The handbook has a formula table for it, but its worked values do not match its own example, so we left it out rather than publish a number that might be wrong. Use the formula your college or the university notice gives you.

FAQ

What is the grade point for each grade in SPPU?

O is 10, A+ is 9, A is 8, B+ is 7, B is 6, C is 5, D is 4, and F or Ab is 0, per the SPPU credit framework handbook.

How is SGPA calculated?

Multiply the grade points of each course by its credits, add them up, and divide by the total credits of the semester.

Is there a CGPA to percentage formula here?

No. The handbook formulas conflict with its own worked example, so this page does not publish a conversion.
