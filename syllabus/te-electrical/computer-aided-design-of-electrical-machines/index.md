## Course outcomes

1. CO1 Design the core and overall structure of a three-phase transformer based on given specifications.
2. CO2 Calculate and evaluate transformer parameters such as resistance, reactance, losses, efficiency, and regulation.
3. CO3 Determine main dimensions and winding details of a three-phase induction motor.
4. CO4 Analyze performance characteristics of induction motors including losses, efficiency, and no-load current.
5. CO5 Apply fundamental design concepts of special machines (PMSM, SRM) and implement CAD tools for machine design.

## FAQ

**How many units are in Computer Aided Design of Electrical Machines?**

Computer Aided Design of Electrical Machines (PEC-321C-ELE) has 5 units: Unit I Transformer Design: Part I (8 h); Unit II Transformer Design: Part II (8 h); Unit III Three phase Induction Motor: Part I (8 h); Unit IV Three phase Induction Motor: Part II (8 h); Unit V Introduction to Special machine design (8 h).

**What is the marks scheme for Computer Aided Design of Electrical Machines?**

The official Electrical Engineering 2024 pattern syllabus lists continuous comprehensive evaluation (CCE) for 30 marks and the end-semester exam for 70 marks, for 3 credits.

**What should I know before Computer Aided Design of Electrical Machines?**
