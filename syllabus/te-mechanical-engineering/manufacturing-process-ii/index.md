## Course outcomes

1. CO1 DECIDE the appropriate cutter, method for gear cutting and able to DETERMINE the cutting parameter using indexing method.
2. CO2 CATEGORIZE the polymer process according to product and able to DEVELOP concept for composite fabrication.
3. CO3 DETERMINE the suitable modern machining process for particular application and EXAMINE the material removal rate.
4. CO4 CLASSIFY the different 3D printing techniques and able to DEVELOP a product by using the 3D printing techniques.
5. CO5 DESIGN and DEVELOP the jig or fixture according to need of the component used in machining.

## FAQ

**How many units are in Manufacturing Process-II?**

Manufacturing Process-II (PCC-304-MEC) has 5 units: Unit I Multipoint cutting tools and Gear Manufacturing (8 h); Unit II Polymer and composite Processing (7 h); Unit III Modern Machining Processes (7 h); Unit IV Additive Manufacturing (7 h); Unit V Jig and Fixture Design (7 h).

**What is the marks scheme for Manufacturing Process-II?**

The official Mechanical Engineering 2024 pattern syllabus lists continuous comprehensive evaluation (CCE) for 30 marks and the end-semester exam for 70 marks, for 3 credits.

**What should I know before Manufacturing Process-II?**

Prerequisite listed in the syllabus: Workshop Practices, Manufacturing Processes-I.
