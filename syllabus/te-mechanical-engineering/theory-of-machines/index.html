UNIT I

Fundamentals of Mechanism

7 hours

Introduction: kinematic link, kinematic pair, kinematic chain, types of constrained motions, types of joints, mechanism, machine, Degree of freedom and Mobility. Inversions of Mechanisms: Four-Bar Chain, Slider crank Chain, Double slider crank Chain. Mechanisms with Higher pairs: Equivalent Linkages and their Cases (Sliding Pairs in Place of Turning Pairs, Spring in Place of Turning Pairs, Cam Pair in Place of Turning Pairs)

UNIT II

Kinematic Analysis of Mechanisms: Analytical Method & Graphical

7 hours

Method Analytical methods: Displacement, velocity and acceleration analysis of slider crank Mechanism. Vector and Complex Algebra Methods (Theoretical Treatment only). Graphical methods: Displacement, velocity and acceleration analysis of mechanisms by Relative Velocity Method (Mechanisms up to 6 Links), Coriolis component of Acceleration (Theoretical treatment only) Instantaneous Centre of Velocity, Kennedy’s Theorem, Angular Velocity ratio Theorem, Analysis of mechanism by ICR method (Mechanisms up to 6 Links)

UNIT III

Synthesis of Mechanisms

7 hours

Steps in Synthesis: Type synthesis, Number Synthesis, Dimensional synthesis, Tasks of Kinematic synthesis - Path, function and motion generation (Body guidance), Precision Positions, Chebychev spacing, Mechanical and structural errors Graphical Synthesis: Inversion and relative pole method for three-position synthesis of Four-Bar and Single Slider Crank Mechanisms Analytical Synthesis: Three-position synthesis of the Four-Bar mechanism using Freudenstein’s equation

UNIT IV

Kinematics of Gears

8 hours

Introduction: Classification, terminology, law of gearing, involute and cycloidal tooth profile, Spur Gear: path of contact, arc of contact sliding velocity, Interference and undercutting, Minimum number of teeth to avoid interference, Force Analysis (theoretical treatment only) Helical and Spiral Gears: Terminology, Geometrical Relationships, virtual number of teeth for helical gears Bevel Gear & Worm and Worm Wheel: Terminology, Geometrical Relationships Gear Train: Types, Analysis of Epicyclic gear Trains, Holding torque - simple, compound and Epicyclic gear Trains, compound Epicyclic gear Train

UNIT V

Cams & Followers

8 hours

Introduction: Types of cams and followers, and their classification, analysis of standard motions to the follower, Cam Profile: Determination of cam profiles for different follower motions Methods of control: pressure angle, radius of curvature and undercutting. Jump phenomenon of Eccentric cam,

Prerequisite: Engineering Mathematics - I and II, Engineering Physics, Engineering Mechanics, Geometric Modelling & Drafting.

Course outcomes

  1. CO1APPLY kinematic concepts to analyze mechanisms used in engineering applications
  2. CO2ANALYZE displacement, velocity, and acceleration of mechanisms using analytical and graphical methods.
  3. CO3DESIGN and SYNTHESIZE mechanisms for specified motion and path generation problems.
  4. CO4ANALYZE gear systems and APPLY kinematic principles in power transmission applications.
  5. CO5DESIGN cam profiles for required follower motion considering practical constraints

FAQ

How many units are in Theory of Machines?

Theory of Machines (PCC-302-MEC) has 5 units: Unit I Fundamentals of Mechanism (7 h); Unit II Kinematic Analysis of Mechanisms: Analytical Method & Graphical (7 h); Unit III Synthesis of Mechanisms (7 h); Unit IV Kinematics of Gears (8 h); Unit V Cams & Followers (8 h).

What is the marks scheme for Theory of Machines?

The official Mechanical Engineering 2024 pattern syllabus lists continuous comprehensive evaluation (CCE) for 30 marks and the end-semester exam for 70 marks, for 3 credits.

What should I know before Theory of Machines?

Prerequisite listed in the syllabus: Engineering Mathematics - I and II, Engineering Physics, Engineering Mechanics, Geometric Modelling & Drafting.
