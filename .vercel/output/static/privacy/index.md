# Privacy policy

The short version: your study data never leaves your browser, there is no account, and analytics cookies run only if you accept them.

Last updated: 25 October 2026. PrepTracker is an independent student project, not affiliated with Savitribai Phule Pune University.

## Who we are

PrepTracker ([preptracker.manavdev.site](https://preptracker.manavdev.site/)) is an independent study planner for SPPU students. For anything in this policy, contact [sia@manavdev.site](mailto:sia@manavdev.site).

## What stays on your device

PrepTracker has no accounts and no server-side database. The following is stored only in your own browser's local storage:

- Study data: your profile, syllabus progress, tasks, marks, revision state and custom resources.
- Cookie choice: whether you accepted or rejected analytics cookies, so the banner does not keep asking.

- Backups are files you export yourself; they go only where you share or save them.
- Deleting everything: use the reset option in the app's Settings, or clear this site's data in your browser. Both remove every stored key.
- Switching browsers or devices starts fresh unless you restore a backup.

## Cookies and similar storage

This site uses two categories:

- Strictly necessary - the on-device storage described above. The site cannot function without it, so it is always on and is never sent to us.
- Analytics cookies - optional. They measure visit statistics: which pages are viewed, how long visits last, the approximate region (country or city level, worked out by the analytics provider), and the device and browser type. Visits are told apart by a random ID stored in the cookie, so the data is pseudonymous rather than anonymous. They are set only after you press Accept on the cookie banner, and advertising storage and ad personalization stay switched off.

Your choice is remembered on this device. To change it, use the Cookie settings button below to reset the banner, or clear this site's data in your browser.

## Error reports

If something breaks while you use the app or read the library, the site sends a short error report to an error-monitoring provider so the bug can be found and fixed. A report contains the error message, the page address, and your browser and operating system type. How long the monitoring provider keeps reports depends on its own settings and terms.

## Third parties that process data for this site

- Hosting and content delivery - serves the site's files and sees the technical data any web server sees (IP address, browser type, time of request), used to deliver pages and prevent abuse. It also runs a page-performance measurement script that sets no cookies.
- Font providers - the site's typefaces are loaded from external font services when a page opens, so these providers see your IP address, browser type and time of request. This happens before you make a cookie choice.
- Analytics provider - processes the pseudonymous visit statistics described above, only when you have accepted analytics cookies.
- Error-monitoring provider - receives the error reports described above.

Each provider processes only what its service needs, under its own privacy terms. Data may be processed on servers outside India; what safeguards apply is set out in each provider's own privacy terms and settings. How long each provider keeps data depends on its own settings and terms, which this policy does not fix.

## What PrepTracker does not do

- No accounts, sign-ups or passwords.
- No advertising, ad trackers, profiling or selling of data.
- No collection of names, emails, phone numbers or precise locations.
- No sharing of your on-device study data with anyone - it never reaches a server.

## Your rights

Most of your data never leaves your device, so you can exercise most rights directly: view or delete your stored study data and cookie choice at any time through the app's Settings or your browser settings.

India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 sets out rights to access, correct and erase personal data and to grievance redressal. Under the Government of India's notification of 13 November 2025, those provisions start 18 months after that date, so they are not yet in force. This page does not decide whether or how the Act applies to this site. To ask about your data, such as the analytics and error data described above, email [sia@manavdev.site](mailto:sia@manavdev.site).

## Children

PrepTracker is a general study tool and does not knowingly collect personal data from children. Since there are no accounts and analytics is opt-in, no age-specific data is gathered.

## Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the "last updated" date at the top changes with it. Material changes are also flagged in the app for a reasonable time.

## Contact and grievances

Questions, requests or complaints: [sia@manavdev.site](mailto:sia@manavdev.site). The Data Protection Board of India is set up under the Act, but its complaint provisions are not yet in force.
