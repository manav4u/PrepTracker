## Course outcomes

1. CO1 Classify different types of structures and determine static and kinematic indeterminacy of beams, frames, and trusses.
2. CO2 Analyze statically indeterminate beams, frames, and trusses using the consistent deformation method and unit load method for various loading and support conditions.
3. CO3 Apply approximate methods such as Cantilever and Portal methods to analyze multi-storey, two-bay rigid frames.
4. CO4 Apply slope-deflection and moment distribution methods to analyze indeterminate beams and rigid jointed frames, including both sway and non-sway conditions.
5. CO5 Apply the stiffness method (structure approach) to analyze beams and rigid frames using matrix formulation for systems with up to three degrees of indeterminacy.
