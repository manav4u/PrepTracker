UNIT I

Introduction to Building Technology

8 hours

Derived reading outline. Source text split at semicolons, line breaks and sentence boundaries, not an official topic hierarchy.

  • Definition and scope of building technology, fundamentals of building construction, classification of buildings as per NBC (National Building Code).Type of structures: load bearing & framed structures and their requirements, type of loads and their effects.
  • Building components and their functions: a. foundation, types of foundations and their suitability, b. wall construction techniques: masonry, c. framed structures components: footing, column, beam, lintels, arches, slabs, precast panels, d. flooring and roofing: types and methods, e. openings: doors and windows with types and installation

UNIT V

Building Bye-Laws and Drawings

8 hours

Derived reading outline. Source text split at semicolons, line breaks and sentence boundaries, not an official topic hierarchy.

Prerequisite: Basic & Engineering Science Course.

Course outcomes

  1. CO1Understand Building Construction Technology – Demonstrate knowledge of fundamental building construction concepts, including structural and non-structural components.
  2. CO2Explain Conventional Building Materials techniques.
  3. CO3Summarize Modern Construction Techniques.
  4. CO4Apply Green Building Concepts – Integrate principles of green building planning and sustainable materials into construction projects.
  5. CO5Describe Building Bye-laws & Regulations – Interpret and apply building bye-laws, regulations, and drawing standards, particularly in line with UDCPR, Maharashtra State.

FAQ

How many units are in Building Construction and Materials?

Building Construction and Materials (PCC-203-CVL) has 5 units: Unit I Introduction to Building Technology (8 h); Unit II Conventional Building Materials and Techniques (8 h); Unit III Modern Construction Techniques (8 h); Unit IV Green Building planning and Sustainable Materials (8 h); Unit V Building Bye-Laws and Drawings (8 h).

What is the marks scheme for Building Construction and Materials?

The official Civil Engineering 2024 pattern syllabus lists continuous comprehensive evaluation (CCE) for 30 marks and the end-semester exam for 70 marks, for 3 credits.

What should I know before Building Construction and Materials?

Prerequisite listed in the syllabus: Basic & Engineering Science Course.
