## Course outcomes

1. CO1 Identify the type of signals and perform elementary operations on signals.
2. CO2 Classify systems based on their properties
3. CO3 Understand fundamental properties of LTI systems and be able to determine response of the system for given input.
4. CO4 Analyze and design of an LTI systems using Fourier transform and Laplace transform.
5. CO5 Apply the concept of Z-Transform to solve difference equations and to find the system stability.

## FAQ

**How many units are in Signals and Systems?**

Signals and Systems (PCC-202-INC) has 5 units: Unit I Fundamentals of Signals (8 h); Unit II Fundamentals of Systems (8 h); Unit III Fourier analysis of Continuous Time Signals (8 h); Unit IV Laplace Transform (8 h); Unit V Z-Transform (8 h).

**What is the marks scheme for Signals and Systems?**

The official Instrumentation and Control Engineering 2024 pattern syllabus lists continuous comprehensive evaluation (CCE) for 30 marks and the end-semester exam for 70 marks, for 3 credits.

**What should I know before Signals and Systems?**

Prerequisite listed in the syllabus: Differential equation, matrix algebra, complex numbers..
