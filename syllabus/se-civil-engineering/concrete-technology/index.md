## Course outcomes

1. CO1 Describe the composition, properties, and functions of various ingredients of concrete.
2. CO2 Understand standard tests on fresh and hardened concrete and interpret results related to workability, strength, and durability.
3. CO3 Design concrete mixes using IS 10262 for various applications, including performance based requirements.
4. CO4 Analyze durability concerns in concrete and recommend suitable preventive or mitigation measures.
5. CO5 Compare and evaluate advanced concrete, sustainable materials, and smart technologies for modern construction.

## FAQ

**How many units are in Concrete Technology?**

Concrete Technology (PCC-253-CVL) has 5 units: Unit I Fundamentals of Concrete Technology (6 h); Unit II Properties of Fresh and Hardened Concrete (6 h); Unit III Concrete Mix Design (6 h); Unit IV Durability and Sustainability of Concrete (6 h); Unit V Advanced and Special Concretes (6 h).

**What is the marks scheme for Concrete Technology?**

The official Civil Engineering 2024 pattern syllabus lists continuous comprehensive evaluation (CCE) for 30 marks and the end-semester exam for 70 marks, for 3 credits.
