Process and Thread Management

9 hours

Course outcomes

  1. CO1Analyze the fundamentals of Operating Systems, including types, structures, system calls, and basic Linux commands.
  2. CO2Apply process scheduling and synchronization to optimize CPU utilization in modern operating systems.
  3. CO3Identify the mechanism for dealing with deadlocks and concurrency concerns.
  4. CO4Apply techniques of memory management to solve memory management problems
  5. CO5Illustrate I/O and file management policies.

FAQ

How many units are in Operating Systems?

Operating Systems (PCC-203-CSE) has 5 units and 45 hours of theory: Unit I Introduction to Operating System (9 h); Unit II Process and Thread Management (9 h); Unit III Interprocess Communication and Deadlock (9 h); Unit IV Memory Management (9 h); Unit V File and Disk Management (9 h).

What is the marks scheme for Operating Systems?

The official Computer Science and Engineering 2024 pattern syllabus lists continuous comprehensive evaluation (CCE) for 40 marks and the end-semester exam for 60 marks, for 3 credits.

What should I know before Operating Systems?

Prerequisite listed in the syllabus: Data Structure, Digital Electronics.
