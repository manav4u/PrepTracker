## Course outcomes

1. CO1 Classify units, standards, performance characteristics of pressure, temperature, Flow, Level, Force, Torque, Displacement, and Speed Transducers
2. CO2 Summarize the working principles and applications of pressure, temperature, Flow, Level, Force, Torque, Displacement, Speed, Vibration, Viscosity and Density Transducers.
3. CO3 Select suitable sensors/transducers for physical parameter measurement.
4. CO4 Use dead weight and vacuum gauge tester to calibrate pressure gauges.
5. CO5 Examine characteristics of T/C, RTD and LVDT. Implement signal conditioning circuits for Thermocouple and RTD for given specification.
