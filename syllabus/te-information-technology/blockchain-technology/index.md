## Course outcomes

1. CO1 Understand the fundamentals of blockchain and distributed ledger technologies.
2. CO2 Describe cryptographic techniques and transaction verification mechanisms used in blockchain systems.
3. CO3 Analyze consensus protocols and cryptocurrency operations.
4. CO4 Analyze blockchain platforms and smart contract architectures.
5. CO5 Evaluate blockchain applications, challenges, and emerging trends.

## FAQ

**How many units are in Blockchain Technology?**

Blockchain Technology (PEC-361C-IT) has 5 units and 45 hours of theory: Unit I Foundations Of Blockchain And Distributed Ledger Technology (9 h); Unit II Cryptographic Foundations Of Blockchain (9 h); Unit III Consensus Mechanisms And Cryptocurrency Systems (9 h); Unit IV Blockchain Platforms And Smart Contracts (9 h); Unit V Blockchain Applications, Challenges And Emerging Trends (9 h).

**What is the marks scheme for Blockchain Technology?**

The official Information Technology 2024 pattern syllabus lists continuous comprehensive evaluation (CCE) for 30 marks and the end-semester exam for 70 marks, for 3 credits.

**What should I know before Blockchain Technology?**

Prerequisite listed in the syllabus: Distributed System, Data Structure.
