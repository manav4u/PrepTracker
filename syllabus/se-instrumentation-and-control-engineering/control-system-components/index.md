UNIT II

### Sequencing & Interlocking for Motors

8 hours

Derived reading outline. Source text split at semicolons, line breaks and sentence boundaries, not an official topic hierarchy.

- Standard symbols used in motor control wiring diagrams.

## Course outcomes

1. CO1 Select switches, relays & contactors for industrial control applications.
2. CO2 Design and construct motor control circuits using standard symbols and understand motor protection techniques.
3. CO3 Examine and interpret pneumatic and hydraulic components, and circuits to develop effective control systems.
4. CO4 Demonstrate the use of power control elements, electrical fuse & circuit breakers, and actuators.
5. CO5 Assess and justify the selection and sizing of control valves for gas, vapor, and liquid services, including the use of positioners for enhanced performance.
