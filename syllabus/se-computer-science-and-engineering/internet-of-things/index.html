Course outcomes

  1. CO1Understand fundamental and ecosystem of IoT.
  2. CO2Interface different sensors and actuators with IoT development boards.
  3. CO3Illustrate different layers of IoT protocols.
  4. CO4Use of cloud and its services.
  5. CO5Apply and develop domain specific IoT applications.

FAQ

How many units are in Internet of Things?

Internet of Things (MDM-271-CSE) has 5 units and 30 hours of theory: Unit I Introduction to IoT (6 h); Unit II Introduction to Sensors, Actuator (6 h); Unit III Protocols for IoT (6 h); Unit IV Cloud for IoT (6 h); Unit V IoT Applications (6 h).

What is the marks scheme for Internet of Things?

The official Computer Science and Engineering 2024 pattern syllabus lists continuous comprehensive evaluation (CCE) for 40 marks and the end-semester exam for 60 marks, for 2 credits.

What should I know before Internet of Things?

Prerequisite listed in the syllabus: Digital Electronics and Logic Design.
