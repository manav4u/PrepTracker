## Course outcomes

1. CO1 Understand the fundamental concepts, culture, principles, and practices of DevOps.
2. CO2 Explain microservices architecture, cloud-native development, containerization, and orchestration technologies used in DevOps environments.
3. CO3 Comprehend and implement Continuous Integration (CI) and Continuous Delivery (CD) concepts, strategies, and pipelines.
4. CO4 Analyze and compare various stages of Continuous Deployment, testing strategies, and deployment pipeline practices.
5. CO5 Evaluate monitoring systems, Site Reliability Engineering (SRE) practices, and apply modern DevOps tools such as Git, Docker, Kubernetes, Jenkins, Ansible, Selenium, Prometheus, Jira, and ELK Stack in software development and operations.

## FAQ

**How many units are in DevOps?**

DevOps (PEC-361A-IT) has 5 units and 45 hours of theory: Unit I Introduction To Devops And The Culture. (9 h); Unit II Microservices Architecture And Cloud Native Development. (9 h); Unit III Continuous Integration And Test-Driven Development. (9 h); Unit IV Continuous Deployment And Orchestration. (9 h); Unit V Continuous Monitoring And Site Reliability. (9 h).

**What is the marks scheme for DevOps?**

The official Information Technology 2024 pattern syllabus lists continuous comprehensive evaluation (CCE) for 30 marks and the end-semester exam for 70 marks, for 3 credits.

**What should I know before DevOps?**

Prerequisite listed in the syllabus: Software Engineering.
