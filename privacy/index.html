Privacy

The short version: your study data never leaves your browser, there is no account, and visit statistics run only if you allow them.

Last updated: 22 October 2026. PrepTracker is an independent student project, not affiliated with Savitribai Phule Pune University.

Your study data stays on your device

Your profile, syllabus progress, tasks, marks, revision state and custom resources are stored in your browser's local storage on your own device. They are never sent to a server, because PrepTracker has no server and no accounts.

  • Backups are files you export yourself; they go only where you share or save them.
  • Deleting everything: use the reset option in the app's Settings, or clear this site's data in your browser. Both remove every stored key.
  • Switching browsers or devices starts fresh unless you restore a backup.

Visit statistics (optional)

With your permission, PrepTracker counts visits with Google Analytics 4 (property G-PWDXVCDCP8) to see which pages get used. This is off until you choose:

  • Before you choose, Analytics runs with storage denied: no cookies are set and no identifier is stored.
  • Allow stats lets Analytics set its cookies and measure usage. No thanks keeps it denied.
  • Your choice is stored in your browser (key pt_ga_consent) and applies to the app and every syllabus library page.
  • To change your mind, clear this site's data in your browser; the consent note will appear again on your next visit.

Google's own terms apply to Analytics: Google Privacy Policy.

What PrepTracker does not do

  • No accounts, sign-ups or passwords.
  • No advertising, ad trackers or selling of data.
  • No collection of names, emails, phone numbers or precise locations.
