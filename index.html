PrepTracker stores your work in this browser. It is not cloud-synced or app-encrypted. Clearing browser data can remove it. Export a private backup from Settings, and restore it when you need to.

Before you begin.

Is every course ready to track?

No. The library labels tracking-ready courses and holds drafts or unresolved source metadata as reference only.

Does it save across devices?

Not automatically. Your work is local to this browser. Export a backup from Settings to keep a private copy or restore it elsewhere.

Is the marks estimate an official result?

No. The marks ledger is an FE estimate. It explains its credit coverage and source contradictions, and does not confirm pass eligibility.
