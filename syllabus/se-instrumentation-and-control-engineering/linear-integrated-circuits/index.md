## Unit-wise syllabus

UNIT I

### Closed loop Op- Amplifier circuits

8 hours

Derived reading outline. Source text split at semicolons, line breaks and sentence boundaries, not an official topic hierarchy.

- Introduction to feedback amplifiers, Loading effect.
- Voltage series feedback (non-inverting amplifier with feedback): deriving close loop gain, input impedance, output impedance and bandwidth
- Voltage follower and its applications.
- Voltage shunt feedback (Inverting amplifier with feedback): deriving close loop gain, input impedance, output impedance and bandwidth
- Inverter circuit, Differential amplifier with one op-amp.
- With derivation of close loop gain.

Voltage shunt feedback (Inverting amplifier with feedback): deriving close loop gain, input impedance, output impedance and bandwidth; Inverter circuit, Differential amplifier with one op-amp. With derivation of close loop gain.

UNIT V

### Active filters and Regulators

9 hours

Derived reading outline. Source text split at semicolons, line breaks and sentence boundaries, not an official topic hierarchy.

- Filters: Definition, types and Difference between active and passive filters, their merits and demerits.
- Filter terminology: Pass band, stop band, cut off, Ripple, Q and order of the filter.
- Butterworth approximations, Low pass (LP), High pass (HP), Band pass (BP), Narrow band pass, Band reject, Notch filter, First and second order filters, (Design of LP, HP and BP filter).

## Course outcomes

1. CO1 Execute the applications of closed-loop Op-Amp circuits in feedback amplifiers to design and analyse various configurations.
2. CO2 Implement linear and non-linear applications of Operational amplifiers to develop integrated circuits for diverse functionalities.
3. CO3 Use timers and special purpose ICs effectively to create multivibrators and oscillators for different electronic systems.
4. CO4 Demonstrate the design and implementation of active filters and regulators to enhance the performance of electronic circuits.
