## Unit-wise syllabus

UNIT I

### Logic Families

6 hours

Derived reading outline. Source text split at semicolons, line breaks and sentence boundaries, not an official topic hierarchy.

- Logic families: TTL, CMOS, RTL, DTL logic family.
- Digital IC specification terminology.
- CD40XX /74XX series digital ICs.

- Interfacing of logic family.
- Tristate Logic, Comparison of Different logic families.

Logic families: TTL, CMOS, RTL, DTL logic family. Digital IC specification terminology. CD40XX /74XX series digital ICs. Characteristics of TTL logic family. Interfacing of logic family. Tristate Logic, Comparison of Different logic families.

UNIT II

### Number system & Logic circuit minimization techniques

6 hours

Derived reading outline. Source text split at semicolons, line breaks and sentence boundaries, not an official topic hierarchy.

UNIT IV

### Sequential Logic

8 hours

Derived reading outline. Source text split at semicolons, line breaks and sentence boundaries, not an official topic hierarchy.

- Flip Flops: Study of SR, JK, MSJK, D, T types of flip flop, excitation tables, Conversion of Flip Flop.
- Counters: Binary, BCD, Up-Down counters.
- Design of synchronous and asynchronous counter using T and D flip flops, Realization of counters using ICs 7490, 7492,7493 and 74193.
- Shift Registers: Serial in Serial out, Parallel in Parallel out shift registers, Ring counter, Johnson counter.
- Programmable logic array (PLA) - Input, Output Buffers, AND, OR, Invert/ Non-Invert Matrix.

## Course outcomes

1. CO1 Execute logical functions using different number systems, Boolean algebra, and minimization techniques to design and implement digital circuits effectively.
2. CO2 Implement various logic families, analyze their performance specifications, and demonstrate Proficiency in interfacing TTL and CMOS logic.
3. CO3 Use combinational and sequential logic circuits to solve complex problems, differentiate between different types of flip-flops, and relate the design of counters and registers with practical applications.
4. CO4 Test and compare the applications of programmable logic devices in digital clock and frequency counter circuits, showcasing their versatility and efficiency.
