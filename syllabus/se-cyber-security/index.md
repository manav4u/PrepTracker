# SE Cyber Security syllabus

Official PDF conflict: reference only.

The curriculum tables and detailed course pages in the same official 2024 PDF disagree. This page preserves the detailed theory-course text. Do not treat it as a confirmed semester plan; verify with your college.

- PCC-203: table says Basics of Computer Network; detailed page says Discrete Mathematics.
- MDM-221: table says Fundamentals of Internet of Things; detailed page says Digital Electronics and Logic Design.
- PCC-252: table says Discrete Mathematics; detailed page says Advanced Data Structures.
- PCC-253: table says Introduction to Cyber Security; detailed page says Probability and Statistics.
- MDM-271: table says Smart Devices and IoT Security; detailed page says Internet of Things.

PCC-202 is listed as theory in the table, but its detailed page has practical-only assessment. The replacement courses have no matching detailed units here. Tracking stays unavailable until a corrected source resolves this.

Second Year, SPPU 2024 pattern. Pick a subject for its units, marks, outcomes and books. Practical, lab and project courses are not listed here, only theory courses.
