## Course outcomes

1. CO1 ANALYZE humanoid robot technologies, social robot applications, and case studies of Atlas, ASIMO, and Pepper.
2. CO2 EXPLAIN swarm basics, smart robots and their applications, and robotics for warfare.
3. CO3 DESCRIBE HRI fundamentals, collaborative robots, and Cobot applications and technology.
4. CO4 IDENTIFY Industry 4.0 fundamentals, IoRT, and its applications across industrial, healthcare, agriculture, smart city, and consumer sectors.
5. CO5 CLASSIFY NLP techniques and automated reasoning methods according to their functions and applications.

## FAQ

**How many units are in Advances in Robotics and Artificial Intelligence?**

Advances in Robotics and Artificial Intelligence (PCC-304-RAI) has 5 units: Unit I Humanoid Robotics and Social Robots (7 h); Unit II Swarm Robotics and Smart Robots (7 h); Unit III Human-Robot Interaction (HRI) and Collaborative (7 h); Unit IV Industry 4.0 and Internet of Robotic Things (IoRT) (7 h); Unit V Natural Language Processing (NLP) and Automated (7 h).

**What is the marks scheme for Advances in Robotics and Artificial Intelligence?**
