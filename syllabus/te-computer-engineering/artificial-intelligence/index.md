## Course outcomes

1. CO1 Explain fundamental concepts of Artificial Intelligence, Agents and ethical implications of AI in real-world scenarios. •
2. CO2 To formulate real-world problems as state-space search models and implement appropriate heuristic, local, and online search algorithms •
3. CO3 To model real-world competitive and constraint-based problems and implement adversarial search algorithms and CSP techniques •
4. CO4 To represent real-world knowledge using propositional and first-order logic and apply Inference techniques and derive logical conclusions in knowledge-based AI systems. •
5. CO5 To apply AI planning techniques for solving classical planning problems and Relate them to real-world industrial AI applications.

## FAQ

**How many units are in Artificial Intelligence?**

Artificial Intelligence (PCC301COM) has 5 units and 45 hours of theory: Unit I Introduction to AI and Intelligent Agents (9 h); Unit II Problem Solving : State Space Approach and Search Strategies (9 h); Unit III Adversarial Search and Game theory (9 h); Unit IV Knowledge representation using Logical Formalisms, Propositional and First order Predicate Calculus (9 h); Unit V Planning and Industrial Applications of AI (9 h).

**What is the marks scheme for Artificial Intelligence?**

The official Computer Engineering 2024 pattern syllabus lists continuous comprehensive evaluation (CCE) for 30 marks and the end-semester exam for 70 marks, for 3 credits.

**What should I know before Artificial Intelligence?**

Prerequisite listed in the syllabus: Data Structures.
