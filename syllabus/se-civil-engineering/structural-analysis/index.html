Prerequisite: Fundamentals Mathematics , Engineering Mechanics and Mechanics of Structures.

Course outcomes

  1. CO1Classify different types of structures and determine static and kinematic indeterminacy of beams, frames, and trusses.
  2. CO2Analyze statically indeterminate beams, frames, and trusses using the consistent deformation method and unit load method for various loading and support conditions.
  3. CO3Apply approximate methods such as Cantilever and Portal methods to analyze multi-storey, two-bay rigid frames.
  4. CO4Apply slope-deflection and moment distribution methods to analyze indeterminate beams and rigid jointed frames, including both sway and non-sway conditions.
  5. CO5Apply the stiffness method (structure approach) to analyze beams and rigid frames using matrix formulation for systems with up to three degrees of indeterminacy.

FAQ

How many units are in Structural Analysis?

Structural Analysis (PCC-252-CVL) has 5 units: Unit I Fundamentals of Structure and Analysis of Redundant Beams (8 h); Unit II Analysis of Redundant Frames (8 h); Unit III Slope-Deflection Method (8 h); Unit IV Moment Distribution Method (8 h); Unit V Stiffness Method (8 h).

What is the marks scheme for Structural Analysis?

The official Civil Engineering 2024 pattern syllabus lists continuous comprehensive evaluation (CCE) for 30 marks and the end-semester exam for 70 marks, for 3 credits.

What should I know before Structural Analysis?

Prerequisite listed in the syllabus: Fundamentals Mathematics , Engineering Mechanics and Mechanics of Structures.
