# SPPU syllabus statistics 2026

133 cataloged theory courses, 662 units, 383 credits and 5231 theory hours across 14 SPPU 2024-pattern year-branch catalogs, computed from the same syllabus data that powers the subject pages. A course taught in several branches counts once per branch catalog. Data date 2026-11-09.

## Key findings

1.

PrepTracker catalogs the official SPPU 2024-pattern syllabus for 14 year-branch catalogs: 9 Second Year and 5 Third Year. One more branch (SE Automobile) is published by SPPU only as a draft and is marked as such.

[Link to this finding](https://preptracker.manavdev.site/research/sppu-syllabus-statistics-2026/#f-coverage)
2.

Across those catalogs, the library holds 133 cataloged theory-course entries, 662 units and 383 credits of theory, copied from the official SPPU syllabus PDFs. A course taught in more than one branch counts once per branch catalog.

[Link to this finding](https://preptracker.manavdev.site/research/sppu-syllabus-statistics-2026/#f-courses)
3.

The most common course size is 5 units: 130 of 133 theory courses (98%, rounded) have exactly 5 units.

[Link to this finding](https://preptracker.manavdev.site/research/sppu-syllabus-statistics-2026/#f-units)
4.

The largest catalog is TE Computer Engineering with 17 theory courses and 85 units; the smallest is SE Cyber Security with 7 courses and 35 units.

[Link to this finding](https://preptracker.manavdev.site/research/sppu-syllabus-statistics-2026/#f-branch-size)
5.

The First Year section covers 10 subjects with 50 units and 240 named topics, 34 credits in total, from the SPPU FE 2024-pattern syllabus.

[Link to this finding](https://preptracker.manavdev.site/research/sppu-syllabus-statistics-2026/#f-fe)
6.

Cataloged theory hours total 5231 across the final catalogs, summed from the per-unit hour allocations in the syllabi (semester hours, not weekly). Each branch page links the official SPPU PDF its numbers come from.

[Link to this finding](https://preptracker.manavdev.site/research/sppu-syllabus-statistics-2026/#f-hours)

## Methodology

- Source: the official SPPU syllabus PDFs, one linked at the bottom of every branch page. Counts are computed at build time from the catalog that renders those pages, so this page cannot drift from them.
- Included: theory courses on branches whose 2024-pattern syllabus SPPU has published as final, and the ten First Year subjects.
- Counting rule: courses are cataloged per branch and year, so a course taught in several branch catalogs counts once per catalog (133 entries, fewer unique titles). Hours are semester hours summed from each unit's hour allocation in the syllabus, not weekly hours.
- Excluded: SE Automobile (SPPU publishes it as a draft; its page is labeled draft), practical, lab and project courses, and the 2019-pattern TE pages kept for reference.
- Percentages are rounded to whole numbers; counts are exact.
- The data date is when the catalog last changed, not when the page was rebuilt.

## FAQ

**How many SPPU branches does PrepTracker cover?**

14 year-branch catalogs with final SPPU 2024-pattern syllabi: 9 Second Year and 5 Third Year. SE Automobile is published by SPPU as a draft and is labeled draft on its page.

[Link to this answer](https://preptracker.manavdev.site/research/sppu-syllabus-statistics-2026/#faq-1)

**How many courses and units are cataloged?**

133 cataloged theory-course entries and 662 units across those catalogs, plus 10 First Year subjects with 50 units. A course taught in several branches counts once per branch catalog; practical, lab and project courses are not counted.

[Link to this answer](https://preptracker.manavdev.site/research/sppu-syllabus-statistics-2026/#faq-2)

**Where do these numbers come from?**

Every count is computed at build time from the same syllabus data that renders the subject pages, and that data is copied from the official SPPU syllabus PDF linked at the bottom of each branch page. Nothing is estimated.

[Link to this answer](https://preptracker.manavdev.site/research/sppu-syllabus-statistics-2026/#faq-3)

**How often do these numbers change?**

When SPPU publishes a new or revised syllabus and the catalog is updated. The date on this page is the data date, not a page rebuild date.

[Link to this answer](https://preptracker.manavdev.site/research/sppu-syllabus-statistics-2026/#faq-4)
