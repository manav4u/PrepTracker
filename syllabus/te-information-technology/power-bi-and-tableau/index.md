## Course outcomes

1. CO1 To Understand the fundamentals of Business Intelligence and Data Analytics tools.
2. CO2 To Perform data cleaning, transformation, and visualization using Power BI.
3. CO3 To Design interactive dashboards and analytical reports using Tableau.
4. CO4 To Apply data analysis techniques for solving business and engineering problems.
5. CO5 To Develop complete visualization solutions for real-world datasets.

## FAQ

**How many units are in Power BI & Tableau?**

Power BI & Tableau (PEC-362A-IT) has 5 units and 45 hours of theory: Unit I Introduction To Business Intelligence And Data Visualization (9 h); Unit II Power BI Fundamentals (9 h); Unit III Advanced Power BI and Dashboard Design (9 h); Unit IV Tableau Fundamentals And Visualization Techniques (9 h); Unit V Advanced Tableau And Industry Applications (9 h).

**What is the marks scheme for Power BI & Tableau?**

The official Information Technology 2024 pattern syllabus lists continuous comprehensive evaluation (CCE) for 30 marks and the end-semester exam for 70 marks, for 3 credits.

**What should I know before Power BI & Tableau?**

Prerequisite listed in the syllabus: 1.Basic knowledge of Computer Fundamentals 2.Understanding of Database Management Systems (DBMS) concepts 3. Basic knowledge of MS Excel and Spreadsheet operations.
