## Course outcomes

1. CO1 APPLY metal cutting mechanics and tool wear analysis to optimize machining processes.
2. CO2 DESIGN gating systems, risers, and ANALYZE casting defects for efficient metal casting processes.
3. CO3 COMPUTE forces, power, and deformation in rolling, forging, and sheet metal operations.
4. CO4 COMPARE welding techniques, interpret weld symbols, and EVALUATE defects for quality joining.
5. CO5 SELECT appropriate grinding and finishing processes based on surface finish requirements

## FAQ

**How many units are in Manufacturing Processes-I?**

Manufacturing Processes-I (PCC-252-MEC) has 5 units: Unit I Theory of metal cutting (8 h); Unit II Metal Casting Technology (8 h); Unit III Metal Forming Technology (8 h); Unit IV Joining Technology (6 h); Unit V Finishing and Fine finishing processes (6 h).

**What is the marks scheme for Manufacturing Processes-I?**

The official Mechanical Engineering 2024 pattern syllabus lists continuous comprehensive evaluation (CCE) for 40 marks and the end-semester exam for 60 marks, for 3 credits.

**What should I know before Manufacturing Processes-I?**

Prerequisite listed in the syllabus: Manufacturing Practice Workshop, Material Science and Metallurgy, Engineering Physics.
