# CCE, End-Sem and term work: how the marks are earned

The revised First Year 2024 pattern syllabus book (effective 2025-26) has a guidelines section for examination scheme. This page restates it in plain words, with nothing added.

CCE 40 End-Sem 60 Term work 25

## CCE: 40 marks, run by your college

Comprehensive Continuous Evaluation covers all five units and is held at institute level.

| Part | Marks | Units |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Unit test | 12 | Units I and II |
| Assignments or case study | 12 | Units III and IV |
| Seminar presentation, open book test or quiz | 6 | Unit V |
| Mini project, project-based learning or group activity | 10 | Any unit |

The book's example timeline: Units I and II in weeks 1 to 4 with the unit test in week 5, Units III and IV in weeks 6 to 8 with assignments collected in week 9, Unit V in weeks 10 to 12 and the seminar, open book test or quiz in week 13. The unit test is built on Bloom's taxonomy levels. For the seminar, you give a talk on a Unit V topic and answer questions, with slides and a 2 to 3 page summary.

## End-Sem: 60 marks, set by the university

A written theory paper on all five units, scheduled by the university, with question papers delivered through QPD (Question Paper Delivery). It is designed on Bloom's taxonomy, from remembering up to creating.

The revised book allocates 12 marks per unit across the five units for the 60, with questions set on Bloom's taxonomy levels.

## Term work: 25 marks, from your journal

Term work is continuous assessment of the journal of practical assignments, with a reflection on each. The book's evaluation criteria:

| Criterion | Weight |
| --- | --- |
| Completeness | 20% |
| Quality of work | 40% |
| Organization | 20% |
| Presentation | 10% |
| Creativity and engagement | 10% |

The book's sample timeline has the journal finalized in week 10 and submitted in week 11. The final term-work grade goes to SPPU at the end of the semester.

See the [scheme and credits tables](https://preptracker.manavdev.site/syllabus/first-year-scheme-credits/) for which courses carry which marks, and the [grading page](https://preptracker.manavdev.site/syllabus/grading-system-sgpa/) for how they turn into grades.

## FAQ

**How is the 40 marks CCE divided in SPPU 2024 pattern?**

Unit test 12 marks on Units I and II, assignments or case study 12 marks on Units III and IV, a seminar, open book test or quiz for 6 marks on Unit V, and a mini project or group activity for 10 marks on any unit.

**Who conducts the End-Sem exam?**

The university. It is a 60 mark written paper on all five units, and the question paper is delivered through QPD.

**What decides the term work marks?**

Your journal of practical assignments, judged on completeness, quality, organization, presentation and creativity, as described in the syllabus book.
